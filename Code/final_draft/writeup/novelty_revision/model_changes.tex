\section*{Economic interpretation}
Switching toward berry in the previous period can renew the appeal of plain
this period. Novelty $N$ is a binary, one-period recovery event for the flavor
switched away from. It is not a first-ever purchase indicator, a continuous
measure of unfamiliarity, or a boost for the flavor switched toward.
The coefficient remains unrestricted: a positive coefficient supports the
proposed recovery mechanism; a negative coefficient implies the opposite.

\section*{Definition and timing}
Let $w_{ijs}$ denote flavor $j$'s share of purchased yogurt packages in calendar
week $s$. Dominance means a strict majority, $w_{ijs}>1/2$.
For two consecutive observed purchase weeks $s-1,s$, define
\begin{align*}
 E_{is}&=\mathbf 1\{\exists k:w_{ik,s-1}=0,\ w_{iks}>0\},\\
 T_{ijs}&=\mathbf 1\{w_{ij,s-1}>1/2,\ \exists k\ne j:w_{iks}>1/2\},\\
 A_{ijs}&=\mathbf 1\{T_{ijs}=1\ \text{or}\ (E_{is}=1\ \text{and}\ w_{ijs}<w_{ij,s-1})\}.
\end{align*}
The revised predictor is $N_{ijt}=A_{ij,t-1}$, provided purchase baskets
are observed in all three consecutive calendar weeks $t-2,t-1,t$; otherwise
$N_{ijt}=0$. Thus the first two observed weeks cannot receive the boost.
A gap resets event eligibility, but does not erase accumulated stock.

A takeover rewards the displaced majority flavor. An introduction rewards
only flavors whose shares decline. Multiple triggers still give $N=1$, not
multiple bonuses. A majority lost to a tie, without an introduction or another
flavor gaining a majority, does not trigger novelty. No event means no boost,
even if the previous event's basket composition persists.

\section*{Utility and unchanged stock}
\begin{align*}
 H_{ijt}&=\sum_{s<t,\ s\text{ observed}}w_{ijs},\\
 V_{ijt}&=\beta_0+\beta_j+\alpha_i p_{ijt}
                +\gamma_i N_{ijt}+\phi_i H_{ijt}.
\end{align*}
The outside utility normalization and optional price-control residual are
unchanged. The three random coefficients retain their current distributions.
Current purchases update stock and the switching event only after current
predictors are recorded. Histories are constructed before likelihood exclusions.
The stock does not decay: recovery enters as a separate temporary utility term.
A positive $\phi_i$ represents reinforcement; a negative one represents satiation.
With binary $N$, $\gamma_i$ is the utility difference when the event turns on,
holding stock and other predictors fixed.

\section*{Comparison and server execution}
The legacy predictor is $1-w_{ij,t-1}$ when last week's purchase is observed.
It remains available as \texttt{previous\_share}; the new option is
\texttt{lagged\_switch\_away}. The dedicated shell selects the new option
and reestimates both controlled and uncontrolled heterogeneous-history demand
using the prepared sample and numerical settings saved in an existing rebuild.
No raw-data rebuild or pricing exercise is run. Earlier pricing dynamics have
not been adapted to this new event rule.
