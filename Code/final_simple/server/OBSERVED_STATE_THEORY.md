# Binary theory analogue with observed initialization

Run on the research server, from the updated project checkout:

```bash
sbatch Code/final_simple/server/run_observed_state_theory.sh
```

The default is the converged no-habit fit from job 50281568. A different server
location can be supplied as the single argument. The job does not refit demand.
It reconstructs the fit's observed-week sample and verifies its household count,
week count, and likelihood before producing results.

For each fitted household, h0 is its **first actual observed yogurt basket**,
captured before price-based likelihood exclusions. Zero-yogurt weeks are skipped.
For the focal flavor, any positive focal purchase sets h0=j; no focal purchase
in that yogurt basket sets h0=0. A mixed basket containing that flavor is inside.
An unmatched/invalid initial basket is unknown: that household is excluded and
counted in the audit, rather than treating it as outside or moving to a later week.

Period 1 and period 2 are the next two consecutive **purchase weeks** after h0,
not necessarily consecutive calendar weeks. Calendar gaps and zero-yogurt weeks
do not advance this pricing time index. Their prices are
optimized, and their choices are generated by the model. Observed later choices
contribute to demand estimation, not to predetermined counterfactual paths.

The binary theory uses the no-habit gamma distribution and fixed empirical alpha
and flavor beta. The uniform firm observes history but not type or quintile;
the informed firm also observes quintile. Both retain unchanged type beliefs
after either outcome. The initial objective integrates persistent-type outcomes
jointly, matching the reported revenue.

The population gamma distribution is held identical across initial histories,
as specified. Consequently, all households with the same h0 face the same
uniform policy, and state-conditional prices do not depend on how many households
start in that state. Actual initial-state frequencies determine overall revenue,
welfare, expected prices, and revenue contributions in the combined population.
Restoring observed h0 alone does not change the state-conditional pricing panels.

Quintiles are equal-probability bins of the fitted population distribution.
This run does not recover household-specific gamma or rank household posteriors.
Such recovery would be a different evaluation specification and is not silently
introduced by the observed-state initialization.

Only recover the new job's `RESULTS_ONLY` directory. It contains:

- `supply_review.pdf`: four pricing decisions and percentage welfare comparisons.
- `revenue_contributions_by_quintile.pdf`: contributions conditional on h0.
- `observed_initial_state_counts.csv` and `initialization_audit.json`.
- `observed_initialization_comparison.csv`: metrics averaged using observed h0 counts.
- `observed_initialization_percentage_changes.csv`: informed-minus-uniform changes
  divided by the absolute uniform baseline.
- `observed_initialization_revenue_contributions.csv`: quintile contributions
  combining both observed initial states.
- Parameter provenance, solver checks, theoretical integration nodes, and figures.

No household records or identifiers are exported. Raw inputs and reconstructed
histories remain in server memory. A new output directory is used for every job.

The earlier local `supply_review/novelty_only_results` figures were generated
from population parameters and hypothetical initial-state panels. They are
benchmarks, not outputs of this observed-initialization server job.
