# Population-parameter benchmark

These local results do not read household first observed yogurt baskets. They evaluate
the binary model separately at h0=j and h0=0 under the fitted population gamma
distribution. They must not be described as an observed-household-initialized run.

The corrected observed-state server workflow is documented in
`Code/final_simple/server/OBSERVED_STATE_THEORY.md`.

Under the maintained identical gamma distribution across h0, restoring observed
initial-state frequencies affects combined-population aggregation, not the
state-conditional optimal policies themselves.
